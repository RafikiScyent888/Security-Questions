## **Security+ (V8) exam objectives summary**

### **General Security Concepts (16%)** 

  - **Compare and contrast various types of security controls:** Understand how layered security controls work together to protect systems and data. 
  - **Demonstrate the impact of change management processes on security:** Understand how planning, testing, approvals, and documentation help maintain secure environments. 
  - **Explain the importance of using appropriate cryptographic solutions:** Apply encryption, certificates, hashing, digital signatures, and key management practices. 

### **Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks (24%)** 

  - **Compare and contrast common threat actors and motivations:** Understand who conducts attacks and why organizations are targeted. 
  - **Explain common threat vectors and attack surfaces:** Identify how attacks target users, systems, applications, networks, cloud resources, and supply chains. 
  - **Analyze indicators of malicious activity:** Recognize signs associated with malware, social engineering, credential attacks, and other security threats. 
  - **Examine common vulnerabilities and associated risks:** Evaluate vulnerabilities and help determine mitigation priorities. 
  - **Explain the security risks associated with artificial intelligence (AI):** Identify risks related to AI systems, models, prompts, privacy, and data exposure. 

### **Security Architecture (19%)** 

  - **Compare and contrast security implications of different architecture models:** Understand security considerations across cloud, hybrid, on-premises, and operational technology environments. 
  - **Apply security principles to secure enterprise infrastructure:** Support secure system design, access controls, segmentation, and connectivity. 
  - **Understand concepts related to data protection:** Protect sensitive information throughout its lifecycle. 
  - **Explain the importance of resilience and recovery in security architectures:** Support continuity through backups, redundancy, recovery planning, and testing. 

### **Security Operations (27%)** 

  - **Apply common security techniques to computing resources:** Help secure endpoints, networks, applications, and data. 
  - **Explain asset management concepts and practices:** Manage hardware, software, and data assets throughout their lifecycle. 
  - **Analyze indicators of vulnerabilities and implement appropriate mitigation strategies:** Identify, prioritize, remediate, and validate vulnerabilities. 
  - **Describe security monitoring and alerting concepts:** Use logs, alerts, dashboards, and security tools to identify potential threats. 
  - **Manage identity and access controls:** Support authentication, authorization, federation, and privileged access management. 
  - **Explain the importance of automation and orchestration in security operations:** Improve efficiency through automation, integrations, and AI-assisted workflows. 
  - **Implement incident response activities:** Support preparation, detection, containment, recovery, and lessons learned. 
  - **Use data sources to support investigations:** Analyze evidence and security data during investigations. 

### **Security Program Management and Oversight (14%)** 

  - **Define elements of an effective security governance program:** Apply policies, standards, procedures, and security guidance. 
  - **Manage security risk management processes and concepts:** Assess, communicate, and manage organizational risk. 
  - **Explain third-party risk management concepts:** Evaluate and monitor vendor and partner security risks. 
  - **Support security compliance and privacy considerations:** Support regulatory, legal, compliance, and privacy requirements. 
  - **Compare and contrast audits and assessments:** Participate in audits, reviews, testing, and assessments. 
  - **Promote security awareness practices:** Help strengthen organizational security through education and awareness.
